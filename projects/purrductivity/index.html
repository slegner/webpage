Purrductivity

Start small. Stay focused. Let the cats come to you.

Purrductivity is a cozy focus app where you study alongside an orange cat, and slowly build a space that attracts even more cat companions.

In collaboration with Run2Achieve Ltd .

How it works

  • Focus for 5 minutes to earn a tuna biscuit
  • Use tuna biscuits to buy decorations for your study room
  • Every 25 minutes, a cat may join your session
  • Different decorations attract different cats

Track your progress with simple stats on focus time and session history, and get personalized suggestions based on your health data from your Apple Watch.

Privacy

Purrductivity stores all of your data — timer state, tuna count, items, photos, discovered cats, and session history — locally on your device. Nothing is sent to any server, and there are no third-party trackers or ads. If you opt in to HealthKit, the app reads heart rate variability and sleep data only on-device to power the Body & Focus view; this data never leaves your phone.

Frequently asked questions

1. Can I switch off the notification when I leave the app? Yes, you can turn off reminders in the menu.

2. Can I add or modify activities? Yes — go to Statistics in the menu and pull down to the bottom. You can add, delete, or rename activities from there.

3. Can I rename the cats? Yes — tap a cat's head icon in the Cat Album to rename it.
